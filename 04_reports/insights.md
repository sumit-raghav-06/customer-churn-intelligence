# 📌 Business Insights Report

## Executive Summary

This project analyzed customer churn behavior using SQL, exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualization, and machine learning techniques. The objective was to identify key factors influencing customer churn and support customer retention decision-making.

---

# 🗄 SQL Analysis Insights

### Contract Type Analysis
Customers with month-to-month contracts exhibited the highest churn rates, indicating that shorter commitments are associated with lower customer retention.

### Monthly Charges Analysis
Customers with higher monthly charges showed increased churn likelihood, suggesting pricing sensitivity may impact customer satisfaction.

### Tenure Analysis
Customers with shorter tenure were significantly more likely to churn compared to long-term customers.

---

# 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis Insights

### Churn Distribution
The dataset shows a moderate churn imbalance, with retained customers forming the majority of the customer base.

### Contract vs Churn
Month-to-month customers displayed substantially higher churn behavior than customers with one-year or two-year contracts.

### Monthly Charges vs Churn
Customers paying higher monthly charges were more likely to leave the service.

### Tenure vs Churn
Customers with lower tenure demonstrated significantly higher churn probability.

---

# 🤖 Machine Learning Insights

A Logistic Regression model was developed to predict customer churn based on customer demographic and service-related features.

### Model Performance
The model achieved strong prediction accuracy and successfully identified a large number of customers likely to churn.

### Confusion Matrix Findings
- The model effectively identified loyal customers
- A significant number of churn-risk customers were successfully detected
- Some churn cases were missed, indicating opportunities for future model improvement

---

# 💼 Business Recommendations

- Encourage customers to shift toward long-term contracts
- Improve retention strategies for new customers
- Review pricing structures for high monthly charge customers
- Use predictive models proactively for churn prevention campaigns

---

# ✅ Conclusion

This project demonstrates a complete business analytics workflow combining SQL, data analysis, visualization, and machine learning to solve a real-world customer retention problem.
